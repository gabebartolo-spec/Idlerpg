# Three-faction global warfare — future multiplayer pillar

**Owner direction, 9 October 2026:** Add a Horde/Alliance/third-team *style* global conflict as a major reason to return and see what happened. Use **three original, visually distinct factions**, never the names, lore, emblems, races or art of Warcraft factions. This is a planned product pillar, **not shipped gameplay**.

## Player experience

The player affiliates their persistent adventurer with a faction; guildmates should be able to fight together. The three factions compete asynchronously for territory, supply routes and strongholds over real server-run campaigns. The player's adventurer can contribute while offline through an assigned role: assault, garrison defence, scouting, supply/crafting, healing or preparation. Preparations, party/build specialisation and meaningful counters matter more than manual taps or money spent.

**The returning-player hook:** a concise illustrated war report tells the player which territory changed hands, which real battles resolved, what their adventurer did, what their faction gained or lost, a notable friendly/rival contribution and **one meaningful next choice**. The watchable 3D world can illustrate a few authored encounters, but campaign results come exclusively from verified server simulation. Do not fake opponents or fabricate player actions. If there is not enough human population, show an honest NPC practice/campaign preview rather than passing bots off as people.

Offer one readable faction map, current campaign progress, personal/guild contribution, upcoming scheduled resolution and the previous campaign's archive/history. Avoid a permanently intrusive war overlay, number-vomit stats, daily chore checklists or forced viewing.

## Fairness and population design

- Three factions are a thematic direction, not a guarantee that 3 sufficiently populated teams will exist at launch. **Measure launch population, concurrency, time zones, faction distribution and guild alignment** before selecting campaign sizes. Start with a small honest test/shard; scale or defer live war rather than fake concurrency.
- Population advantage is not player skill. Test active-population normalisation, scoring caps/diminishing returns, time-zone weights and earned underdog catch-up carefully; do not hand an unannounced victory to a trailing side. Keep methods disclosed and auditable.
- **No permanent snowball:** periodic score/campaign reset, a durable personal history of achievements, new objectives and bounded victory rewards. Review how faction choice and guild switching interact; keep friends/guildmates together where possible and prevent mid-war faction hopping for easy rewards.
- A player on the losing faction should still earn useful individual contribution rewards and have a meaningful next goal. Winner prestige can include titles, banners, cosmetic trophies and earned gacha tokens, but avoid winner-takes-all resource domination.
- Balance across roles: free/low-power participants can supply, defend, scout or prepare without being made irrelevant by overpowered damage builds. Damage, gold and XP purchased boosts should **never directly buy war score, extra attempts, territory or victory**. Compare real full-entitlement free/light/high spender outcomes and normalize combat budgets where required.
- Anti-exploit and server authority: no client-authored contribution events, no duplication on reconnect/offline catch-up, no botting/AFK point farming, no impossible instant travel, no adversarial guild transfers, no fake scoreboards. Resolve once with a deterministic history and audit log.
- Keep a non-compulsory rhythm: meaningful changes and summary results worth checking, **not** mandatory real-time attendance or a countdown that forces the player to log in at 3am. Record outcomes even while the app is closed. Optional push only for meaningful moments and user-controlled preferences.
- PvP communication should be moderated/controlled before open chat, with player privacy and reporting safeguards.

## Reference mechanics and caution

- **Guild Wars 2 World vs. World:** three teams contest objectives over accumulated campaign/skirmish scoring; ArenaNet now periodically creates population-balanced WvW teams, including guild grouping. https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/World_versus_world ; official support: https://help.guildwars2.com/hc/en-us/articles/32834740484755-WvW-Matchmaking
- **The Elder Scrolls Online Alliance War:** three-alliance campaign, objective-based score, hourly evaluation and explicit trailing-side bonuses. https://help.bethesda.net/app/answers/detail/a_id/7965/~/how-are-ava-campaigns-scored-in-the-elder-scrolls-online
These are mechanical inspirations, **not evidence a three-way MMO campaign will work with a small independent game's live population**.

## Scope, dependencies and pilot acceptance

Dependencies: R28 account/identity, R29 trusted authoritative progression, R30 friends/guild social safety, R18 real guilds, R31 real asynchronous raid / role proof, R33 competitive fairness, R34 guild war pilot, R35 event operations, R43 population simulations. Treat as a **post-foundation R44 concept**, not an excuse to delay fixing the first 15 minutes of solo play.

A minimal pilot must show **three actual accounts or three small actual groups**, one short asynchronous campaign with real faction contributions, a server-reconciled territory result, a player-readable catch-up report and fair loss-side rewards. Test uneven 1:2:5 active populations, dormant factions, guild grouping, time-zone gaps, full paid-benefit stacking, tampering, retries and save restoration. Do not claim a healthy live war or retention benefit from a few synthetic players. Measure **war report openings, voluntary return intent, role participation, losing-side return and subsequent real D7/D30 cohorts**, alongside overall enjoyment and any pressure to check in.

## Commercial PvP positioning (9 October)

The owner expects progression PvP to attract substantial gacha-token purchasers seeking competitively meaningful power. Provide a visibly power-based progression duel/campaign role where gear/companions and rarity upgrades have real effects, and a separate equalised-ranked mode. Strong spenders require worthy peers and rivals; avoid automatically winning all fights or harvesting hopeless free-player matches. One player's purchased strength may improve a frontline result but must not directly buy territory, war points, extra ranked attempts or certain campaign victory. Audit free/light/high-spend power, win probability and population/losing-side retention with [MONETISATION_STUDY.md](MONETISATION_STUDY.md) and R33/R43.
