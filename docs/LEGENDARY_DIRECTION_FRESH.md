# Fresh Legendary direction

The owner rejected the blue-metal/gold-trim crown, eclipse and heart-furnace
concepts as poor and too close to Epic gear. Those studies and their brief have
been removed from the branch.

`../art/imported/references/fresh_legendary_direction.svg` begins a new design
family with three visibly separate material languages:

- **Cinderwake / Crownblade:** a broad flowing volcanic-glass edge with one
  ember seam and a rough wrapped bone grip. No gold, bright metal or gem.
- **Shellward / Starforged Helm:** a low open hood made from layered pale shell
  plates, bound with raw red fiber. A deep face opening defines its silhouette.
- **Rootbound / Titanheart Plate:** russet cloth under a one-sided bark mantle,
  with hand-laced shingle plates and ties. It reads as natural armor rather than
  a metal cuirass dressed with a colored stone.

These are fresh visual studies, not a Tripo model, in-game gear or owner-approved
Legendary art. The names are working names; the existing catalogue item IDs and
stats remain the integration targets. The immediate art test is whether the
silhouettes and materials feel unmistakably rarer and more memorable beside the
current Epic examples at portrait scale. Refine the direction from that review
before turning it into source model prompts.

The built-in raster image generator is unavailable in this session. The board is
a hand-authored SVG so the distinct shapes, material breaks and color families
can be inspected and revised directly without adding paid generations. Its PNG
preview lives in the chat's `outputs/legendary-pilot/` review folder.
