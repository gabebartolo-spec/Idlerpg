# Legendary direction — second pass

Status: proposed direction, awaiting owner art review. The existing three Tripo
meshes are Epic-level pipeline samples and do not establish the target quality.

The goal is recognition at actual phone scale: a player should identify the
item from its outline before reading its name. Use the existing hero and world
as the style reference. Broad shapes, readable bevels, restrained colour and
one clear focal feature take priority over dense ornament.

| Item | Signature | Construction and fit | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crownblade | An unequal, split crown tip with a visible cleft | One rigid sword; crown is part of the blade, hilt remains readable in the hand; worn bronze and storm iron with one amber seam | A normal straight sword dressed in extra gems; disconnected floating shards |
| Starforged Helm | An offset eclipse arc rising from one side | Arc physically joins the shell; open face, hollow interior, clearance around the existing head; dark shell with a narrow warm rim | Generic horn symmetry; an oversized ring that dominates the whole hero; a filled face cavity |
| Titanheart Plate | A central heart furnace framed by broad ribs | Wearable shell around the tapered torso; open neck and arms; ribs frame one amber core without replacing the hero skeleton | A flat gem pasted onto a generic cuirass; shirt clipping; excessive tiny filigree |

## Review before another paid generation

Compare the proposed concept sheet with the existing hero at phone size.
Approve or revise the three signatures before spending credits. Prepare one
isolated item reference per job once the direction is settled; a three-item
sheet is a review aid, not a clean single-model input.

## Review after generation

Keep the original source and provenance, decode if needed, fit in Blender and
preserve editable files. Render neutral front/side views, then equip the real
hero. Capture idle, walk, attack and gear icons. Judge silhouette, shirt/head
clearance, hand grip and material consistency in the actual game. Budget targets
remain 3k/4k/5k triangles and 1024 texture maps for this pilot. Android performance
requires a device check; desktop captures cannot substitute for it.

Effects come after shape approval. A subtle furnace pulse or restrained edge
accent can support identity, but extra glow cannot rescue a generic silhouette.
