# Lanternwood earned pursuits

Three longer journeys extend the existing region. Each has five authored encounters and requires no live choices or simultaneous attendance. They run once per deliberate selection, then return to normal adventuring. They do not auto-repeat or require consecutive days.

| Journey | Unlock | Duration | Guaranteed missing gear | First / repeat final gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mothwatch | Clear Lantern Hollow | 2 hours | Mothglass Spear | 160 / 65 |
| Moonwell | Clear Keeper's Rise | 8 hours | Mooncap Mantle | 220 / 90 |
| Lamplighter's Circuit | Clear Keeper's Rise | 24 hours | Lamplighter Seal | 340 / 140 |

Each route also has a twelve-gold cache banked when encountered. Final gold and missing gear are secured in a fixed saved chest, delivered only when opened. An owned item or one already secured in another chest is not promised twice. A later successful journey replaces a sold item; these replaceable world items yield zero summon tokens. Appearance ownership remains permanent after selling functional gear.

New original models/icons: cream moth-wing spear with moon-glass blade, blue/purple mooncap mantle and amber root-framed seal. Spear and mantle also unlock permanent wardrobe options. The seal displays as functional accessory equipment. Chest reveals show actual stats/effect name and offer direct Equip.

## Three earned loadouts

All use the guaranteed opening Wolfskin Hood. No banner item or paid rescue is required.

| Build | Functional gear / talent | Starting-level ATK / HP | Trail distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Striker | Mothglass Spear + Thick hide (one level-two talent point) | 15 / 51 | Opportunist doubles attack reduction against telegraphed keeper strikes |
| Guardian | Goblin Cleaver + Mooncap Mantle | 10 / 59 | Hardened reduces all trail damage by 25%, rounded up |
| Lamplighter | Goblin Cleaver + Lamplighter Seal | 13 / 51 | Thornward halves telegraphed damage |

Existing combat effects remain active in ordinary fights. Expeditions snapshot attack, health, Thornward, Hardened and Opportunist when departing. Equipment changes during a trip do not alter that frozen preparation. Higher levels improve these starting-level examples.

The scripted trials obtain the new gear by actually completing/opening its routes using guaranteed cleaver/hood and a boss-earned Briarheart Charm as preparation. The three resulting loadouts survive all three new routes without stew. Earlier route gates and boss discovery are seeded as test preconditions; a complete fresh-save acquisition/progression audit remains required.

## Several-day milestones

Opening all three gear rewards completes Lanternwood keepsakes. Three successful clears of each route complete Glasswing Guardian, Moonwell Keeper and Keeper of the Lamps, each awarding the existing once-only 25 gold plus a selectable permanent profile title. All three masteries entail 102 hours of deliberate journey time, before earlier routes; this is an authored progression horizon, not measured participant engagement. No streak, reset or calendar-day gate is used.

The ordinary expedition screen shows duration, reward, preparation hint and mastery count. Detailed encounter prose and rules stay in the optional Field Guide. Fixed waiting periods alone do not establish that the content is satisfying; the study must assess reward desire and whether players choose to return.
