## Device checks

- [ ] Fresh install opens in portrait; launcher icon and text render correctly.
- [ ] Update the existing install with the same signing identity; existing local progress survives.
- [ ] Complete a short session using touch; navigation, keyboard and large text remain usable.
- [ ] Finish an expedition, return, open its saved chest and wear its actual reward.
- [ ] Suspend/resume, restart and relaunch after process termination; rewards remain single and progress survives.
- [ ] Return after a measured offline interval; catch-up is readable and respects the seven-day cap.
- [ ] Reduced motion and optional audio work on the phone.
- [ ] Create/recover an online account; keep credentials out of recorded evidence.
- [ ] Reach the service over mobile data and Wi-Fi; exercise a connection loss and same-action Retry without duplicate grants.
- [ ] Three independent player accounts join one guild, choose distinct roles and settle one shared raid after its real deadline.
- [ ] Each eligible player opens their own reward once, retries safely and sees their owned appearance after recovery.

Record failures with exact action, expected/actual outcome and build/device. Check boxes only after the actual flow passes.
